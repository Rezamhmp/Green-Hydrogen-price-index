METHODOLOGY

About the Index

This index estimates an indicative competitive selling price for green hydrogen using the empirical green-hydrogen-to-natural-gas price relationship presented in Mahmoodpoor et al. (2026)

The model uses the daily closing price of front-month Henry Hub natural gas futures as the market-linked input and converts it into an indicative hydrogen selling price through the pricing formulation presented in the study.

The formulation, including the underlying assumptions and parameter definitions, is presented in the research article and detailed further in Supplementary Material, Appendix A: "Modeling and Price Estimation of Green Hydrogen." The appendix also provides the supporting derivation and validation of the adopted green-to-gray hydrogen price relationship.

The green-hydrogen production-cost component represented in the study is incorporated through the empirical green-to-grey multiplier. This multiplier reflects the technology and market conditions underlying the research framework and may change as technology, costs, and market conditions evolve.

A producer whose actual production cost is below the indicated price may have greater economic potential, while additional subsidies or incentives may further improve project economics.

The complete mathematical formulation, assumptions, derivation, and supporting references are documented in the associated peer-reviewed research article and supplementary material.
